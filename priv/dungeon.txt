start lobby
fact console.dormant

room lobby
  You are in the neglected lobby of what appears to have once
  been a respectable computer science department.
  A faded sign reads:
  ABANDON ALL FRAMEWORKS, YE WHO ENTER HERE.
  A corridor leads north.
  north corridor
end

room corridor
  You are in a poorly illuminated corridor. The carpet has a
  repeating pattern that may or may not terminate.
  The lobby is south. A red door is east.
  south lobby
  east ruby_room
end

room ruby_room
  Everything here is slightly too elegant. A small red gemstone
  rests on a pedestal.
  The corridor is west. A dubious staircase leads north.
  west corridor
  north segvault if carrying.ruby
end

room segvault
  You stand before THE SEGVAULT. It hums with the smug certainty
  of a memory error that has not happened yet.
  A black console has a suspiciously ruby-shaped socket.
  The staircase is south.
  south ruby_room
end

object ruby
  name ruby
  description The ruby is red enough to make a point of it.
  at ruby_room
  portable
end

object pedestal
  name pedestal
  description The pedestal is far too elegant to be portable.
  at ruby_room
  fixed
end

action use ruby on console
  require carrying.ruby
  say The ruby fits the socket. Somewhere, a machine sounds less dormant.
  clear console.dormant
end
